Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Account data: your email and a hashed password.
  • Your content: documents, FAQ entries and settings you add.
  • Chat data: messages that visitors of your website send to the assistant, the page address, and contact details visitors choose to leave. A random visitor identifier is stored in the visitor's browser (localStorage) to keep the conversation.

Only to provide the Service: to answer visitors, show you conversations and analytics, forward chats to your Telegram if you connect it, and bill you.
